Getting to SMP Tijuana from San Diego

A practical guide to the border crossing, parking, rideshare, and what to bring — for clients from Chula Vista, San Diego, El Cajon, Carlsbad, Escondido, and throughout the county.

Important: Border crossing times, requirements, and procedures change and can vary significantly by day, time, and current conditions. Always check official CBP wait times at cbp.gov before your trip. The information on this page is provided as general guidance only and may not reflect current conditions.

Step-by-Step: How to Get There

1

Check border wait times

Before you leave home, check current pedestrian or vehicle wait times at cbp.gov/wait-times or the CBP One app. Wait times fluctuate throughout the day — early morning and mid-week crossings are typically faster than weekend afternoons.

2

Drive to San Ysidro

Take I-5 South to the San Ysidro exit. If you plan to walk across, park on the US side — there are several paid lots and structures within a few blocks of the pedestrian crossing. Rates vary; arrive early to find convenient parking.

3

Cross into Mexico

Entering Mexico at San Ysidro is generally quick — foot traffic crosses in a few minutes. Follow signs for the pedestrian crossing (El Chaparral / PedWest). No paperwork is required to enter Mexico for a short visit.

4

Rideshare to the clinic

Once across, open Uber or a local rideshare app. The ride to New City Medical Plaza is approximately 5 minutes and costs roughly $3–5 USD (prices fluctuate). You can also walk to the clinic — it's under a mile from the crossing, though the route crosses a busy boulevard.

5

Return crossing

Returning to the US takes longer. Vehicle crossing can take 45–90 minutes or more at peak times; pedestrian crossing typically takes 20–40 minutes. Factor this into your schedule, especially for your first visit. Your SMP session is 2–4 hours, so plan for a half-day trip.

What to Bring

  • Valid US passport or passport card — required to re-enter the United States. A driver's license alone is not sufficient for re-entry.
  • Cash or card in USD — we accept USD. ATMs are available on both sides of the border.
  • Your appointment confirmation (WhatsApp message is fine)
  • Comfortable clothing — no special preparation required for a consultation or first session
  • A light snack and water if you prefer — sessions can last 2–4 hours
Note on REAL ID and enhanced licenses: A REAL ID-compliant driver's license is sufficient for domestic US travel but is not sufficient to re-enter the US from Mexico by land. You need a valid US passport book or passport card. Check cbp.gov for current accepted documents.
