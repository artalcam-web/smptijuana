Certifications

Master Estefany Ruiz holds 7 international certifications in scalp micropigmentation and advanced pigmentation techniques, obtained through internationally recognized micropigmentation training programs and academies. Certification details are available to review upon request — please ask during your consultation.

About the "Master" designation: In the micropigmentation industry, "Master" refers to a practitioner who has completed advanced-level training and certification across multiple international programs. It is a professional designation within the SMP field, not a government-issued medical or clinical title.

Technique

Estefany works with a precise, dots-based micro-needle application that replicates the visual impression of hair follicles at the scalp surface. The goal is a result that is consistent in density and natural in appearance from all angles under normal lighting.

She works across a range of scalp conditions and treatment areas, including:

  • Receding hairlines and hairline design
  • Crown thinning and overall density work
  • Full bald head coverage (close-shaved appearance)
  • Alopecia areata and totalis
  • Scar camouflage (including FUE/FUT transplant scars)

She operates in English and Spanish. Every consultation and session is bilingual.
