Frequently Asked Questions

Is it safe to visit Tijuana for SMP?

Yes. Our clinic is inside New City Medical Plaza — a professional medical building steps from the San Ysidro border crossing. Thousands of US patients visit Tijuana for medical procedures every month. Your safety and comfort are our priority throughout the process.

Can Americans get SMP in Tijuana?

Absolutely. SMP Tijuana is specifically designed for US clients from San Diego County — Chula Vista, National City, El Cajon, La Mesa, Carlsbad, Oceanside, Escondido, and beyond. Our entire team is bilingual in English and Spanish, and we accept USD.

How much does SMP cost in Tijuana vs. San Diego?

SMP at our clinic starts at $699 USD. The final price depends on the treatment area and is confirmed at your free consultation. Average prices at clinics in San Diego County are typically $2,500 or more.

How do I get there from San Diego County?

Drive to San Ysidro and cross at the border — our clinic is ~5 minutes from the crossing at New City Medical Plaza. You can park on the US side and walk across, or drive directly. Border wait times vary; check CBP One or the San Diego CBP wait times page before you leave. A rideshare from the crossing to our clinic costs ~$3–5 USD.

How long does a session take?

2–4 hours per session. Most clients complete treatment in 2–3 sessions spaced 7–10 days apart — easy to schedule as a day trip.

Is scalp micropigmentation permanent?

Results last 3–5 years before a touch-up is needed. No surgery, no recovery time — you walk out the same day looking sharp.

How does SMP compare to a hair transplant?

SMP is non-surgical, costs 80–90% less than a transplant, and requires zero recovery time. It replicates the look of a freshly shaved head with micro-dots of pigment — ideal if you want immediate results without surgery.

What do I need to cross the border?

A valid US passport or passport card. No visa required for a short medical visit to Tijuana. Walking across the pedestrian crossing takes 5–10 minutes. Return to the US typically takes 10–20 minutes on foot.

Do you speak English?

Yes — our entire team is fully bilingual in English and Spanish. No language barrier, ever.
