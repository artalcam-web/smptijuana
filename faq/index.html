The Procedure
What is scalp micropigmentation?

Scalp micropigmentation (SMP) is a non-surgical cosmetic procedure in which micro-dots of pigment are deposited into the scalp using a fine needle. The result creates the visual impression of a closely shaved head or adds density to thinning areas.

SMP does not grow hair. It is a cosmetic technique — results look most natural when the scalp is kept shaved or very short. Individual results vary depending on skin type, treatment area, number of sessions, and aftercare.

How many sessions will I need?

Most treatments are completed in 2–3 sessions. Sessions are spaced 7–10 days apart to allow for healing between each one. The number of sessions depends on the treatment area, the density you want to achieve, and how your skin responds. Your specialist will give you a realistic estimate at the consultation.

How long does each session take?

Each session typically takes 2–4 hours, depending on the treatment area. A hairline-only session is shorter; full-head coverage takes longer. Plan for a half-day trip from San Diego.

Is scalp micropigmentation permanent?

Results are long-lasting but not permanent. Pigment fades gradually over several years due to natural skin turnover, sun exposure, and individual biology. When fading becomes noticeable, a touch-up session can refresh the result. Individual results vary.

What can I expect after each session?

You will receive written aftercare instructions after each session. General guidelines:

  • Keep the scalp dry for several days after each session
  • Avoid direct sun on the treated area while healing
  • Do not scratch, pick, or rub the scalp during healing
  • Avoid swimming pools, saunas, and steam rooms until healed
  • Apply SPF to the scalp once healed to slow pigment fading

Following aftercare instructions directly affects how pigment settles and how long the result lasts.

Does SMP work for women?

Yes. SMP can address hair thinning in women by adding the visual impression of density along the part line or throughout the scalp. The approach and realistic expectations vary by individual — a free consultation will clarify what is achievable for your specific situation.

Getting Here from San Diego
How do I get to the clinic from San Diego?

Drive or take transit to San Ysidro, cross the border at the San Ysidro Port of Entry, and take a rideshare (Uber or local app) to New City Medical Plaza — approximately 5 minutes from the crossing, roughly $3–5 USD.

Border wait times vary significantly. Always check current times at cbp.gov or the CBP One app before you leave. For a full guide, see our San Diego client travel guide.

What documents do I need to cross the border?

US citizens need a valid US passport book or passport card to re-enter the United States from Mexico by land. A REAL ID-compliant driver's license is not sufficient for land re-entry. Border crossing requirements can change — always verify current requirements at cbp.gov before your trip.
