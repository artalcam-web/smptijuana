What Is Scalp Micropigmentation?

Scalp micropigmentation (SMP) is a non-surgical cosmetic technique in which a specialist deposits micro-dots of pigment into the scalp using a fine needle. The result resembles the look of a freshly shaved head or adds the visual impression of hair density over thinning areas.

SMP does not grow hair. It is a cosmetic procedure — individual results vary depending on skin type, the treatment area, the number of sessions, and aftercare. Results are long-lasting; pigment fades gradually over several years, after which a touch-up session may be desired.

Who is SMP for? Men and women experiencing hair thinning, receding hairlines, pattern baldness (alopecia androgenetica), alopecia areata, or scarring. A free consultation with Master Estefany Ruiz will confirm whether SMP is appropriate for your specific situation.

Aftercare & Healing

Following each session, you'll receive specific aftercare instructions. In general:

  • Keep the scalp dry for the first few days after each session
  • Avoid direct sun exposure on the treated area while healing
  • Do not pick or scratch the scalp during the healing period
  • Avoid swimming pools, saunas, and steam rooms until fully healed
  • Use SPF on the scalp once healed to slow pigment fading over time

Detailed written aftercare instructions are provided after each session. Following them directly affects how the pigment settles and how long results last.

What to Expect from Results

SMP creates the visual impression of a closely shaved head or fuller hair density. It does not add actual hair. Results look most natural when the scalp is kept shaved or very short. Individual results vary — skin type, scalp condition, sun exposure, and aftercare all influence how the result appears and how long it lasts.

Pigment typically fades over 3–5 years, at which point a touch-up session can restore the appearance. This is a predictable characteristic of the procedure, not a defect.
